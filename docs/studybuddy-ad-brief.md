## Part 1 — Project Summary

### What StudyBuddy is

StudyBuddy is a gamified mobile study companion built for Sri Lankan **GCE Ordinary Level (O/L)** and
**GCE Advanced Level (A/L)** students, with a focus on **Tamil-medium** learners who struggle to find
good notes and practice questions in their own language.

It turns studying into a game: you read a lesson, you earn coins. You clear a flashcard set, you earn
more. You pass a quiz, you climb the leaderboard. The app is built around one idea — *Play, Learn, Level Up*.

### Who it's for

- O/L and A/L students in Sri Lanka
- Tamil-medium students first (notes and questions in Tamil), English medium supported
- Students studying alone at home who have no tuition class or study group

### What's inside the app

**Learning content**

- Subject library — Science, Maths, English, Tamil (built as an expandable structure: Subject → Chapter → Lesson)
- Lesson notes written in a short, readable, exam-focused style
- Flip flashcards for fast revision before a test
- MCQ quizzes with instant right/wrong feedback and a score at the end

**Gamification (the hook)**

- **Coins** — +1 for finishing a lesson, +2 for clearing a flashcard set, +5 for passing a quiz
- **Pass mark** — a quiz must be scored 80% or higher to earn coins, so students actually have to learn it
- **Ranks** — Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Champion as coins add up
- **Daily streak** — study every day and the streak counter grows
- **Achievements** — First Note, First Quiz, First Flashcards, 5 Lessons, 3 Quizzes, Coin Miner, 7-Day Streak, Gold Rank
- **Reward shop** — spend earned coins
- **Leaderboard** — compete with other students island-wide

**AI Panda**

- A friendly panda study assistant students can ask questions to
- Answers are grounded in the syllabus content, so it stays relevant to O/L and A/L

**Social & profile**

- Student profile with level (O/L or A/L), medium (Tamil/English), province, bio
- Public profile page with a shareable link and QR code
- Sign in with Google, progress saved and synced across devices

### The look

Deep premium purple, dark-mode-first, with a claymorphic "game UI" feel — soft rounded cards, glowing
badges, coin-burst animations. The mascot is a purple-hoodie gaming panda in a headset. Tagline:

**PROMPT START**

Create a **30-second vertical (9:16, 1080×1920) animated advertisement** for a mobile app called
**StudyBuddy**. Deliver it as a self-contained animated HTML file (inline CSS + JS, no external assets
except the logo I've attached), running on a loop, with every frame timed to the storyboard below. If
an animated HTML file is not possible, deliver a shot-by-shot animatic storyboard with exact timings,
motion notes, and all on-screen text ready for a video editor.

**The product**

StudyBuddy is a gamified study app for Sri Lankan GCE **Ordinary Level (O/L)** and **Advanced Level
(A/L)** students, made especially for **Tamil-medium** students. It has syllabus notes, flip
flashcards, MCQ quizzes, an AI panda tutor, coins, ranks, daily streaks and an island-wide
leaderboard. Tagline: **PLAY • LEARN • LEVEL UP**.

**Audience & tone**

Sri Lankan school students aged 14–19. The tone is energetic, warm and playful — like a mobile game
trailer, not a corporate ad. Fast cuts, bouncy easing, satisfying reward moments. Never dull or
"educational software" looking.

**Visual style**

- Dark premium purple throughout. Use exactly these colours:
  background `#12082B`, cards `#1E103F`, primary `#5B21B6`, accent `#7C3AED`, violet `#8B5CF6`,
  lilac `#C4B5FD`, coin gold `#FBBF24`, cyan highlight `#22D3EE`, streak orange `#FB923C`,
  success green `#34D399`. Hero gradient `#4C1D95` → `#6D28D9` → `#7C3AED`.
- Claymorphic game UI: soft rounded cards (24px radius), inner glow, purple drop shadows,
  floating particles and small pixel sparkles in the background.
- Mascot: the gaming panda from the attached logo — purple hoodie, gaming headset, winking.
  Animate it with subtle bobbing, blinking, and a thumbs-up on the reward beats.
- Motion: spring/bounce easing, coin-burst particle effects, number counters that tick up,
  progress bars that fill, badges that pop in with a flash.

**Language rules (important)**

- All spoken and headline copy is in **Tamil**.
- Keep these words in English on screen: StudyBuddy, O/L, A/L, XP, Quiz, Flashcards, Notes, Coins,
  Rank, Leaderboard, AI Panda, Download.
- Use a clean, bold Tamil font that stays readable at phone size. Tamil text must never be cut off,
  squeezed, or overlapped by other elements — give it generous line height.

**Storyboard — 30 seconds**

**0.0–3.0s — The hook (problem)**
A dark purple screen. A tired student silhouette at a desk, surrounded by scattered paper notes that
float and swirl chaotically. Text punches in, word by word:
Tamil: **"தமிழ் மொழியில் நல்ல நோட்ஸ் கிடைக்கலையா?"**
*(Can't find good notes in Tamil?)*
Sound feel: a rising whoosh.

**3.0–5.5s — The turn**
All the scattered papers get sucked into the centre and compress into a glowing purple phone. The
StudyBuddy panda logo bursts out with a shield flare and a light sweep across the wordmark.
Tamil: **"இனி கவலை இல்ல."** *(No more worries.)*
Then the logo settles with the tagline **PLAY • LEARN • LEVEL UP**.

**5.5–10.0s — Notes & subjects**
Phone mockup, portrait, tilted slightly in 3D. Inside it, a subject grid animates in card by card:
**Science, Maths, English, Tamil** — each card a purple clay tile with a subject icon.
A card is tapped; it opens into a clean lesson-notes screen, text scrolling smoothly.
Tamil overlay: **"O/L & A/L Notes — தமிழ் மொழிமூலத்தில்."**
*(O/L and A/L notes — in Tamil medium.)*

**10.0–14.0s — Flashcards**
The phone shows a flashcard that flips in 3D on the Y axis — question on the front, answer on the
back — then a second, then a third, faster each time, with a swipe-stack effect.
Tamil overlay: **"Flashcards-ல் வேகமாக Revision."** *(Fast revision with flashcards.)*

**14.0–19.0s — Quiz & the reward moment (this is the emotional peak)**
An MCQ quiz screen. Four options; the finger taps one; it snaps green `#34D399` with a checkmark
pop. Score counter ticks up to **80%**. Then a full-screen gold coin burst — dozens of `#FBBF24`
coins exploding outward and raining down, the panda cheering with a thumbs-up.
Big centred number: **+5 COINS**.
Tamil overlay: **"Quiz பாஸ் பண்ணா — Coins உங்களுக்கு!"** *(Pass the quiz — coins are yours!)*

**19.0–23.0s — Streak, rank & leaderboard**
Split into three quick beats, ~1.3s each:
1. A flame icon in `#FB923C` with a counter ticking **1 → 7**, label **"7 DAY STREAK"**, Tamil:
   **"தினமும் படி."** *(Study every day.)*
2. A rank badge morphing Bronze → Silver → **Gold**, glowing on arrival.
3. A leaderboard list sliding up, a purple avatar row climbing from #9 to **#1** with a trophy flare.
   Tamil: **"இலங்கை முழுவதும் போட்டி போடு."** *(Compete across all of Sri Lanka.)*

**23.0–26.5s — AI Panda**
The panda mascot slides in at the side of the phone. A chat bubble appears with a Tamil question
typing out, and the panda replies instantly with a bubble of its own — a small typing-dots animation
before the reply lands.
Tamil overlay: **"சந்தேகமா? AI Panda-கிட்ட கேளு — உடனே பதில்."**
*(Got a doubt? Ask AI Panda — instant answer.)*

**26.5–30.0s — Logo & call to action**
Everything converges into the full StudyBuddy shield logo, centred, with a light sweep and particle
sparkle. Tagline underneath: **PLAY • LEARN • LEVEL UP**.
Below it, a gold `#FBBF24` pill button pulses gently:
Tamil: **"இலவசமாக இப்போதே Download பண்ணுங்க!"** *(Download free, right now!)*
Small line under the button: **O/L & A/L · தமிழ் மொழிமூலம்**
End on the logo held for the final 0.8s.

**Audio direction**

Upbeat lo-fi/chiptune hybrid, 110–120 BPM, building to a drop at the coin-burst at 14s. Layer in
game SFX: card flip whoosh, correct-answer chime, coin cascade, badge unlock sparkle, a soft
button-tap on the CTA.

**Hard requirements**

- 9:16 vertical, 1080×1920, 30 seconds exactly, safe margins of 120px top and 220px bottom so nothing
  gets covered by Reels/TikTok UI.
- The hook must land within the first 2 seconds — no slow intro.
- Every claim shown must be a real feature: notes, flashcards, quizzes, coins, ranks, streaks,
  leaderboard, AI Panda. Do not invent features, do not show fake exam results or grade guarantees.
- Legible at phone size: minimum on-screen text size equivalent to 42px at 1080 width.
- The logo I attached is the final frame — do not redraw or restyle it.
